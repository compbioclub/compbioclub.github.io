The lab’s first travel to Macau in 2025. We had a great lunch buffet at Macau Tower 360 Cafe, and then visited the University of Macau. The team had a great time and enjoyed the food and the view. Also, everyone reached more than 35,000 steps on that day, and made the second highest department record in the CityU walking contest. It was a great, meaningful, fun, and healthy day!
