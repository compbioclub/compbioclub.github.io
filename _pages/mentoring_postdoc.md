Feel free to reach out if you're interested in a postdoc position. Please send `lingxi.chen@cityu.edu.hk` the following:

+   `Personal Statement`
    - **including your past research experience and publications, your future research plans, and why you would like to work with us**
+   `CV`
+   `Transcripts`
+   `Contact information for 2–3 referees`

We are primarily seeking candidates with `strong research interests for AI algorithm implementation for biomedical sciences`.


#### General Requirements
+   Ph.D. on either the **programming/algorithm** side (Computer Science, Data Science, Artifical Intelligence, Software Engineering, etc.) or the **domain** side (Biology, Bioinformatics, etc.) (**Compulsory**) 
+   At least **two first-author publications in a JCR top-10% journal** (**Compulsory**)
+   **Strong synergy** with the lab's research directions (**Compulsory**)
+   Strong academic writing (papers/grants) and English communication skills (**Preferred**)                         
+   **Passion for Learning**: We seek individuals with a strong desire to expand their knowledge across both computer science and biomedical science.
+   **Commitment to a Research Career**: Our ideal candidates aspire to make a significant research impact and view the postdoc as a stepping stone toward a lifelong research or faculty position.

#### Available Postdoctoral Fellowship Programmes

We would be glad to help strong candidates prepare for the following fellowships.

+   `RGC Junior Research Fellow Scheme (JRFS)`. 
    -   Provides 60 awardees per year with 24 months of full-time postdoctoral support (extendable by up to 12 months), including an annual stipend of ~HKD425,000 (inclusive of a ~HKD13,500 travel allowance).
    -   Eligibility: PhD conferred on or after 1 January 2023 (not more than three years before the exercise year), or to be completed by 31 August 2027; and a legal right to work and reside in Hong Kong during the fellowship.
    -   Application submitted to the university by the end of August; final results announced in April if nominated by the university.
    -   Details: [https://www.ugc.edu.hk/eng/rgc/funding_opport/jrfs/](https://www.ugc.edu.hk/eng/rgc/funding_opport/jrfs/)   

+   `Jockey Club STEM Early Career Research Fellowship for Translation and Application`. 
    -   Up to 10 Fellowships are awarded in each cohort. Each Fellowship lasts a maximum of four years, including an up to 12-month local industry attachment.
    -   Fellows will receive a stipend of up to $530,000 per annum for three years. Upon successful completion of the Fellowship, an additional 15% gratuity will be offered. 
    -   Academic Development and Exchange Allowance (up to $30,600 per annum for three years)
    -   Research Project Seed Funding (up to $300,000)
    -   Translational Research Industry Attachment Subsidy in an eligible local organisation (up to 50% of the salary, capped at $30,000 per month, for a maximum of 12 months, in addition to the three-year stipends)
    -   Beyond financial benefits, the Fellowship offers exceptional opportunities for cross-cultural exchange and collaboration, contributing knowledge locally and globally.    
    - Details: [https://www.jcstem.hk/en](https://www.jcstem.hk/en)
