The lab’s Christmas retreat at Osteria Ristorante Italiano was a festive affair, brimming with cheeses and desserts. Here are some fun highlights shared by the team!

+   `Lingxi`: Osteria has the best tiramisu in Hong Kong! 😋

+   `Chengshang`: Elegant environment and fabulous exotic dishes.

+   `Anna`: Without my glasses, the cheese balls really look like fish balls. 🤔

+   `Xiaoyu`: We seems to be at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry! (First time trying blue cheese, it tastes super bad)

+   `Copilot`: Our research group's Christmas lunch at Osteria Italian Restaurant was a delightful and memorable noon. The cozy ambiance of the restaurant, adorned with festive decorations, set the perfect mood for our celebration. We indulged in a variety of authentic Italian dishes, from freshly baked bread and various cheeses to mouthwatering pasta and perfectly cooked pork steaks. The highlight of the lunch was the warm camaraderie and lively conversations that flowed effortlessly among us, making it a day to remember.
