Lab members went on a cycling trip from Tai Wai (大围) to Tai Mei Tuk (大美督)—about 23 km—and completed the ride in roughly 1.5 hours, following the scenic coastal route along Tolo Harbour. After arriving, we visited the Tai Mei Tuk Reservoir main dam (大美督水库长堤) for a short walk and photos.

In the evening, we joined the BMS Department BBQ gathering (繁星烧烤), where we enjoyed grilled meat, hotpot, and great conversations—a relaxing end to an active day.
